This is the original, on-line avatar of The Syllabary.

A print version - The Syllabary: A Poem in 2272 Parts - has been published in two different sequences of 25 copies each. A few of these eleven-volume sets are available at www.molecularpress.com. No further printing is envisaged.

The origin and rationale of this work are discussed in Orasho (Red Squirrel Press, 2022), which can be ordered through www.molecularpress.com.

The Syllabary, its structure, verse and glyphs, are copyright © Peter McCarey, 1997-2023. All rights reserved.

Programming and digital design by Eric Hamlin, 2018 and 2026.


Peter McCarey is grateful to the Rockefeller Foundation for a summer residency at Bellagio in 2015, and to the Hawthornden Castle Trust for a fellowship in the spring of that year.

Thanks also to the Maison Rousseau et Littérature for exhibiting and jointly publishing a paper edition of the work.

Special thanks are due to Richard Price for regularly publishing extracts from the work in Painted, spoken.

Other extracts have appeared in many anthologies and publications, whose editors are thanked.

Parts have been performed in Scotland, Switzerland, Italy, England, France, Portugal, Germany, Russia, Montenegro, Macao/China and the United States of America. The author thanks the translators for their hard work.

The Syllabary is dedicated to Mariarosaria Cardines.
