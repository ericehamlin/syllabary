This is The Syllabary, by Peter McCarey. It is available online, free of charge.

A limited edition of The Syllabary: A Poem in 2272 Parts has been published in two different sequences of 25 copies each. A few of these eleven-volume sets are available at www.molecularpress.com.

On screen, the dials allow users to interrupt a reading and select a different syllable. They can be used also to repeat a given reading.

The Syllabary program itself is stable, but changes in the major operating systems disable it from time to time. Please report any problems you encounter to panjandrum@molecularpress.com
